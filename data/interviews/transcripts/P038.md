**Interviewer:** And just between us, off the record, what do you really think it means for you?

**Interviewer:** How does it feel, picturing that?

## S3 · Fast

**Interviewer:** Now a faster version. Within 2 to 3 years, AI matches skilled professionals at most thinking work. Lots of office and professional workers lose their jobs. **Carlos:** I'd say the people at the top are cashing in while everyone else fights for scraps. Government can't fix it, though. They'll just mess it up worse.

**Interviewer:** And just between us, off the record, what do you really think it means for you?

**Carlos:** Rich people with booming shares order a lot of food, so maybe tips go up. But half the city's laid-off professionals would be on the apps. I'd be competing with former lawyers for a burger run.

**Interviewer:** What would you actually do in the first year? Be as specific as you can.

**Interviewer:** How does it feel, picturing that?

**Interviewer:** You'd say government can't fix it, but you're struggling. Would you take help if it was offered?

**Carlos:** I'd say it was always coming for drivers. Nobody should be surprised.

**Interviewer:** And just between us, off the record, what do you really think it means for you?

**Interviewer:** How does it feel, picturing that?

**Carlos:** Alarmed. I'm used to hustling, but this is hustling with no road.
