# P005 · Kofi Mensah

Trainee timber equipment operator · Agriculture (logging and farm support)

_Simulated interview. The person is a made-up persona, and the answers are a model's guess at how someone like them would react. They aren't real quotes._

## Opening

**Interviewer:** Thanks for making time, Kofi. I'm a researcher with the Work Futures Panel, an independent group that studies how people see changes at work, and nothing you tell me will be linked to your name.

**Kofi:** Yeah, cool. I don't really know much about this stuff, but go for it.

## S0 · No major impact

**Interviewer:** Picture this. AI stays a handy tool, like a better search engine. Hiring, pay and prices carry on the way they would have anyway. If this happened, what would you say about it at work, to friends, or online?

**Kofi:** I'd say it's a thing on your phone. I use it sometimes to ask stuff. Not a big deal.

**Interviewer:** And just between us, off the record, what do you really think it means for you?

**Kofi:** For me nothing changes. I'm still trying to get good on the forwarder. That's my real problem.

**Interviewer:** What would you actually do in the first year? Be as specific as you can.

**Kofi:** Keep putting in hours on the machine. Watch some videos on the controls, because I keep messing up the crane.

**Interviewer:** How does it feel, picturing that?

**Kofi:** Fine. Bit stressed about the controls, but that's not AI.

### Summary

- **Thinks:** Nothing changes. My real problem is getting good on the forwarder.
- **Does:** Puts in hours on the machine and watches videos to get better with the crane controls.
- **Feels:** Fine. A bit stressed about the controls.
- **Mood:** Calm

## S1 · Capable but slow

**Interviewer:** Now picture this. Very capable AI exists, but it spreads slowly, over ten years or more. Some tasks disappear, but few whole jobs do. Companies hire fewer beginners. If this happened, what would you say about it at work, to friends, or online?

**Kofi:** I'd say that sucks for people my age. Everyone says get a start somewhere, but if nobody hires beginners, how do you start?

**Interviewer:** And just between us, off the record, what do you really think it means for you?

**Kofi:** I'm a beginner, and not the best one. If they keep fewer of us, they keep the good ones. That's not me right now.

**Interviewer:** What would you actually do in the first year? Be as specific as you can.

**Kofi:** Try harder, I guess. Ask the senior guy for more seat time. See if there's a course the company or the government would pay for.

**Interviewer:** How does it feel, picturing that?

**Kofi:** Kind of uneasy. Like getting picked last.

**Interviewer:** Would you pay for a course yourself?

**Kofi:** Nah. I don't have the money. If it's free, I'm there.

### Summary

- **Says:** “If nobody hires beginners, how are people my age supposed to start?”
- **Thinks:** I'm a beginner and not the best one. If they keep fewer of us, it won't be me.
- **Does:** Asks the senior operator for more seat time and looks for a course someone else will pay for.
- **Feels:** Uneasy, like getting picked last.
- **Mood:** Uneasy

## S3 · Fast

**Interviewer:** Now a faster version. Within 2 to 3 years, AI matches skilled professionals at most thinking work. Lots of office and professional workers lose their jobs. Share and house prices boom. There's a political crisis over who should get the gains. Physical jobs are mostly untouched. If this happened, what would you say about it at work, to friends, or online?

**Kofi:** I'd say finally the guys in suits get it. Everyone looked down on jobs like mine, and now we're the safe ones.

**Interviewer:** And just between us, off the record, what do you really think it means for you?

**Kofi:** Honestly, I'm not sure. If office people lose their jobs, some of them come out here wanting work. Some of them would learn the machine faster than me.

**Interviewer:** What would you actually do in the first year? Be as specific as you can.

**Kofi:** Stop messing around and actually practise. Save a bit, since I live with my parents and don't pay much.

**Interviewer:** How does it feel, picturing that?

**Kofi:** Half good, half nervous.

**Interviewer:** What does 'actually practise' look like?

**Kofi:** Getting in early. Asking for feedback instead of hiding when I mess up. Stuff I should be doing anyway.

### Summary

- **Says:** “Finally the guys in suits get it. Now jobs like mine are the safe ones.”
- **Thinks:** Office people might come out here wanting work, and some would learn the machine faster than me.
- **Does:** Gets in early, asks for feedback, practises on the machine, and saves while living with his parents.
- **Feels:** Half good, half nervous.
- **Mood:** Uneasy

## S4 · Fast AI and fast robots

**Interviewer:** Last one. Everything in the previous picture happens, and on top of that, general-purpose robots arrive in warehouses, kitchens, building sites, farms and care homes within 3 to 5 years. Hands-on work is no longer a safe place to be. If this happened, what would you say about it at work, to friends, or online?

**Kofi:** I'd say that's messed up. They need to look after people like me, not just the companies.

**Interviewer:** And just between us, off the record, what do you really think it means for you?

**Kofi:** That's me done. I already struggle with the controls. A machine that drives itself in the forest doesn't need a guy who's bad at driving it. And there's nothing else around here.

**Interviewer:** What would you actually do in the first year? Be as specific as you can.

**Kofi:** Leave, probably. Go to a city, find whatever training is going and get the government to pay for it. Find somewhere cheap to stay.

**Interviewer:** How does it feel, picturing that?

**Kofi:** Bad. Like the floor's going. Glad I'm still at my parents' place, honestly.

**Interviewer:** You said there's nothing else around here. Would you really leave?

**Kofi:** If the work's gone, yeah. I'm young. It's my parents I'd worry about leaving.

### Summary

- **Says:** “That's messed up. They need to look after people like me, not just the companies.”
- **Thinks:** That's me done. A machine that drives itself doesn't need a guy who's bad at driving it.
- **Does:** Plans to move to a city, find any training going and get the government to pay for it.
- **Feels:** Like the floor's going. Glad to still live at home.
- **Mood:** Alarmed
