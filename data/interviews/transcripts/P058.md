## S3 · Fast

**Interviewer:** Now a faster version. Within 2 to 3 years, AI matches skilled professionals at most thinking work. Lots of office and professional workers lose their jobs. Share and house prices boom. There's a political crisis over who should get the gains. Physical jobs are mostly untouched. If this happened, what would you say about it at work, to friends, or online?

**Elena:** I'd say very clearly that this is too fast. We don't release a new drug in two years without testing. Why release this?

**Interviewer:** And just between us, off the record, what do you really think it means for you?

**Elena:** If AI matches skilled scientists, then my job becomes checking its work and signing my name. I could live with that. What worries me is that nobody is really checking anything at that speed, in biology especially. That's a safety issue, not a jobs issue.

**Interviewer:** You said it's not a jobs issue for you. Is that right, if AI matches someone at your level?

**Elena:** Mostly right. I'm senior and I'd be the one signing. Someone has to be responsible. But I'll admit I don't fully know. I'm guessing like everyone else.

**Interviewer:** What would you actually do in the first year? Be as specific as you can.

**Elena:** Join a safety group or advise a regulator if they'd have me. Insist our lab keeps human review on anything risky. Put some of the boom gains aside for my daughter.

**Interviewer:** How does it feel, picturing that?

**Elena:** Worried. Not for me. For how careless it could get.

### Summary
